title: "Agnieszka Althaber"
---

I am a sociologist and inequality researcher. What inspires my work is the tension between increasingly rapid social change in individuals’ life courses and surprisingly enduring social structures. Across several current projects, I investigate the consequences of wealth inequalities with a focus on couple and family dynamics as well as gender inequalities.

Methodologically, my research centers on theory-driven quantitative and longitudinal analysis. It builds on my long-standing experience in large-scale survey data collection, expertise in linking data from different sources, and research within interdisciplinary collaborative networks. This profile is complemented by experience in mixed-methods projects and familiarity with qualitative approaches.

I’m currently a postdoctoral research fellow at the [Department of Sociology at LMU Munich](https://www.soziologie.lmu.de/de/das-institut/personen/kontaktseite/agnieszka-althaber-6b3f7c80.html). Previously, I was a researcher at the [Collaborative Research Center “Structural Change of Property”](https://www.sfb294-eigentum.de/en/projects/subprojects/eigentumsungleichheit-im-privaten-II/) at Friedrich-Schiller-University Jena and at the [Berlin Social Science Center (WZB)](https://www.wzb.eu/de/personen/agnieszka-althaber). I was also a visiting researcher with the Women and Work Research Group at the University of Sydney, hosted by [Professor Marian Baird](https://profiles.sydney.edu.au/marian.baird).

My work has appeared in *Kölner Zeitschrift für Soziologie und Sozialpsychologie*, *Journal of Marriage and Family*, *Social Inclusion* and *Social Psychology*. I received my PhD in sociology from Freie Universität Berlin in 2021. My [dissertation](https://shop.budrich.de/produkt/teilzeitarbeit-von-maennern/) examined men’s part-time employment from a life-course perspective, demonstrating the central role of occupational working-time cultures in shaping men’s working hours.
